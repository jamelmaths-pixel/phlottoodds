Privacy Policy

Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 18, 2026

This policy explains what information is collected when you visit phlottoodds.com (“the site”) and how it is used. The site is designed to collect as little information as possible.

Information you give us

The site has no accounts, no sign-up and no forms. You can use every tool on the site without giving any personal information. The calculators run entirely in your own browser.

Information collected automatically

Like almost every website, the site's hosting provider (GitHub Pages, operated by GitHub, Inc.) automatically records basic technical information when pages are requested, such as your IP address, browser type, the page visited and the date and time. This is used to deliver the site, keep it secure and measure overall traffic.

The site loads fonts from Google Fonts. When it does, your browser connects to Google's servers, and Google may receive your IP address.

Cookies and advertising

The site may display advertising served by Google AdSense or other advertising partners. When it does:

  • Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on your previous visits to this and other websites.
  • Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to you based on your visits to this site and other sites on the Internet.
  • You can opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings (adssettings.google.com). You can also opt out of some third-party vendors' use of cookies for personalized advertising at aboutads.info.

For more information, see how Google uses information from sites that use its services: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings. The site's tools keep working without cookies.

How information is shared

We do not sell personal information. Technical information may be processed by the service providers named above (hosting, fonts and advertising) under their own privacy policies.

Children

The site is intended for adults. Lottery games are restricted to adults in the Philippines.

Your rights

If you are in the Philippines, the Data Privacy Act of 2012 gives you rights over your personal data, including the right to be informed, to access and to object. Because the site does not collect personal information directly, most requests concern the service providers listed above.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated, for example when advertising is added. The effective date above shows when it last changed.

Play responsibly. The lottery is entertainment, not an investment. No method, software or past result can raise your chance of winning.
